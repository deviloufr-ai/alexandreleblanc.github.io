How I work

Linear

Fast, opinionated issue tracking that stays out of the team's way.

Speed is a feature

Linear's whole value is that it is fast and low friction, so the team actually keeps it current. I lean into that, with minimal process, keyboard first, and no mandatory fields nobody reads, because a tool people update honestly beats a heavyweight one they route around.

Cycles over endless backlogs

I use Linear's cycles to keep the team focused on a short, committed set of work rather than an infinite list. A tight cycle forces the prioritization conversation weekly, which is exactly where it belongs.

Projects tie issues to outcomes

Individual issues do not tell a story. I group them into projects with a clear goal and target date, so anyone can see how today's tickets ladder up to something that matters. The roadmap and the tracker stay in sync.

Less admin, more building

I resist turning Linear into a bureaucracy. The fewer required fields and manual rituals, the more the team trusts the board. The tool should shrink the overhead of coordination, not add to it.

Real example

On smaller, fast moving teams I have used Linear to run tight weekly cycles with projects mapped to roadmap bets, giving engineers a clean, fast workflow while giving me a truthful view of what is actually shipping.
